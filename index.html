📚 English Articles

A/An (Indefinite) & The (Definite) - Complete Guide

🟢 A/An - Indefinite Article

1. First Mention / Unknown Items
Used with singular countable nouns for things not mentioned before or unknown to the listener.
I saw a dog chasing cars on the street.
Could you give me an apple?
2. Meaning "One"
Can be used to mean "one" or "a single".
I need a minute to think.
3. Meaning "Per/Every/Each"
Used instead of "per", "for every", or "each".
George calls me three times a day.
60 km an hour
10 miles a litre
Once a week
4. Special Uses
Used with names, currency, fractions, measurements, and some diseases.
Currency: a pound, a dollar
Fractions: a third, a half
Measurements: a mile, a kilo
Diseases: a cold, a fever, a headache
️ Exception: NOT used with flu, pneumonia, tonsillitis, etc.
💡 Remember: Use "a" before consonant sounds and "an" before vowel sounds (a university, an hour)

🔴 The - Definite Article

1. Previously Mentioned / Known
Used with nouns (singular/plural, countable/uncountable) when already mentioned or clear from context.
The book that I borrowed from you is interesting.
Pete found a kitten in the park. He took the kitten home with him.
2. NOT Used with Plural Groups
The is NEVER used before plural nouns representing a general group.
Cars did not exist two hundred years ago.
The cars did not exist two hundred years ago. ❌

⚫ No Article

1. Personal Names
No article before personal names.
Celia, Nathan, John, Mary
2. Sports, Games, Days, Months, Holidays
No article before names of sports, games, days of the week, months, holidays, colors, drinks, dishes, and languages.
He's good at basketball.
She speaks Italian very well.
Monday, January, Christmas, red, coffee, pasta
⚠️ Exception: Use "the" if followed by "language" (the Italian language)
3. Countries, Cities, Streets, Parks
No article before most place names.
Countries: Russia, Australia, France
Cities: Milan, London, Paris
Streets: Smith Street, Oxford Street
Parks: Hyde Park, Central Park
Continents: Asia, Europe, Africa
Lakes: Lake Michigan, Lake Baikal
4. Individual Mountains & Islands
No article before single mountains or islands (but use "the" for ranges/groups).
Mount Etna, Mount Everest
Tasmania, Malta, Sicily
⚠️ Exception: Lake Baikal → the Baikal
5. Names with Personal Names
No article when two-word names start with a personal name or place name.
Victoria Station, Moscow University
⚠️ Exception: the Black Swan Hotel
6. Businesses Named After Founders
No article for shops, restaurants, hotels, banks named after founders ending in -s or -'s.
Barclays Bank, Brown's Hotel, Selfridges
7. Institutions (Purpose vs. Building)
No article when referring to the purpose/function of school, university, college, court, hospital, prison, church, bed.
He's been in hospital for two weeks. (as a patient)
⚠️ Exception: Use "the" when referring to the building: They've taken him to the hospital near his house.

🎯 Key Rule:
  • A/An = any one (not specific)
  • The = that specific one (we know which)
  • No article = general/plural/proper names
